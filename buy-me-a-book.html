A few things to know

Do I pay to request a book?

No. Posting a request is free. A donor pays for the book if they choose to help. There is no guarantee that every request will receive a gift.

Do I need a Kindle device?

No. Eligible Kindle gifts can be read in the free Kindle app on a supported phone, tablet, or computer. You need an Amazon account to redeem a Kindle gift.

Can readers in Africa receive Kindle gifts?

It depends on Amazon’s rules for your country and the book. Amazon says recipients must be in the same country as the giver to redeem Kindle gifts. This site does not verify country eligibility. Confirm it before buying. You can also use our free-reading links without waiting for a gift.

What happens after I send a request?

Your request is reviewed before it appears publicly. Save the confirmation code shown after submitting. If someone gifts the book, Amazon emails you a redemption link. After redeeming it, use Confirm a book received to update your request.

Who sees my details?

Approved requests display your name, book, and reason. Donors can see your email to send the gift. Do not include a phone number, home address, or other private information in your reason. Payments happen on Amazon; we do not collect payment details.

How is this site supported?

Some Amazon links are affiliate links. Qualifying purchases may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. You can use the request form and free-reading resources without buying anything.
